Valuation/Inspection of Property

Our company provides comprehensive valuation and inspection services to accurately assess the true worth and condition of various properties. Leveraging over 30 years of expertise, our Managing Director, Anand Behari Sharma, leads a team of skilled professionals in conducting meticulous inspections. We evaluate structural integrity, construction quality, and overall condition of residential, commercial, and industrial properties. Utilizing current market data, we determine fair market value and identify potential risks and liabilities. Our detailed reports, including photographs and expert opinions, meet all insurance and legal requirements, ensuring clients receive accurate and reliable valuations.

×

General Insurance Services

Our company specializes in a diverse range of insurance claims, including motor accident claims for OD, TPBI, and TPPD. We handle engineering claims, burglary claims, and business interruption claims like LOP and MLOP, ensuring clients are covered for unforeseen disruptions. Our services also include managing claims for deterioration of stock, shopkeeper's packages, and domestic insurance needs. We offer comprehensive coverage for workmen compensation, LPG multiperil policies, and personal accident claims. Additionally, we address mediclaims, money-in-transit, fidelity guarantee insurance, and marine claims.

×

Liability Insurance Claims

Our company expertly handles a range of liability insurance claims, including compensation for third-party motor accident claims, both on a fault and no-fault basis. We manage claims related to professional indemnity, public liability, product liability, employers' liability, and director's liability, ensuring efficient resolution and fair compensation.

×

Life Insurance Claims

Our company offers expert handling of life insurance claims, ensuring beneficiaries receive the support and compensation they are entitled to during challenging times. With over three decades of experience, our team provides thorough and compassionate assistance in processing claims, verifying documentation, and coordinating with insurers to expedite payouts.

×

Identification and Handwriting

Our company specializes in advanced identification and handwriting analysis to uncover the authenticity of various documents. We address model forgery, traced forgery, and forgery by impersonation with precise forensic techniques. Our experts detect block forgery, identify typewriting, and validate embossing paper seals and water seals.

×

Fingerprints

Our company provides comprehensive fingerprint analysis services, encompassing latent fingerprints, chance prints, inked prints, block prints, palm prints, foot prints, and shoe prints. We utilize advanced techniques like poroscopy and scale photography to ensure accurate documentation and analysis.

×

Modus Operandi

Our company excels in analyzing the modus operandi of criminal activities through meticulous examination of crime scenes. We investigate entrance and exit routes, analyze tool marks, and conduct site inspections based on the principles of exchange to reconstruct criminal activities accurately.

×

Legal and General

Our company provides expert legal and general support services for insurance and forensic cases. We specialize in recovery through subrogation, court appearances as witnesses, and detailed statement-taking to support investigations and legal proceedings.
